## Description
Lexa helps you understand your legal situation in minutes — in plain language.

Meet the law at a stressful moment — a contract to sign, an eviction notice, a
dispute at work — without the confusion. Lexa turns dense legal language into
clear answers scoped to your country and state.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Ask about your situation and get clear, jurisdiction-aware explanations
• Upload a contract or notice and see the obligations, deadlines, and risky
  clauses explained side by side
• Generate first-draft letters and documents to get started
• Organize everything into matters with deadlines and reminders
• Get an honest read on when a situation needs a professional — and find one

IMPORTANT
Lexa provides legal INFORMATION, not legal advice, and using it does not create
an attorney–client relationship. Laws vary by location and change over time —
always confirm anything important with a licensed lawyer in your area before you
act, sign, or file. Lexa is not a law firm and does not practice law.

Built for people, and the lawyers who help them.
